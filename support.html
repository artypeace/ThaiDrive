A question or answer looks wrong

Tap Report a question under the question. The app prepares an email with the question's reference number so we can find it. Add what seems wrong and send it. You decide whether to send the draft.

Premium is missing after reinstalling or on a new device

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Lifetime and Weekly Premium are both restored this way.

Cancel Weekly Premium or ask for a refund

Weekly Premium renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel, open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Thai Drive and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or refund your payment ourselves.

AI helper, photos and permission

AI explanations and sign recognition are optional. The app asks before anything is shared with the AI service. To withdraw permission, open Settings → Privacy and data in Thai Drive and tap Withdraw permission.

Scans are kept in the AI helper history on your device. You can delete them there. If the camera or photo library does not open, allow access for Thai Drive in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

Progress and statistics

Your answers, streak and statistics are stored on your device. Thai Drive has no accounts. To start over, open Settings in the app and tap Reset study statistics.

Exam reminders do not arrive

Set your exam date in the app, then check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Thai Drive. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.
